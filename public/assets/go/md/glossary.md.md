# GLOSSARY.md

**Path:** `GLOSSARY.md`\
**Role:** Shared project vocabulary and context

## Purpose

Define the terms contributors and agents should use when discussing the product and its code.
Record the distinctions and ownership boundaries that are easy to miss from filenames alone.

## Guidelines

- Give each domain term a concise definition and use that term consistently in code and discussion.
- Explain overloaded words and the differences between concepts that sound similar.
- Point to the relevant modules, contracts, or architecture decisions for implementation details.
- Keep business definitions in their authoritative project when several repositories share them.
- Update definitions when an agreed contract changes; keep historical rationale in decision records.
- Link to `PRODUCT.md` for product goals and `AGENTS.md` for working instructions instead of
  duplicating those documents.
